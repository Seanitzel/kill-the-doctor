# Interior artwork and references

Four original raster assets were created with the built-in image generation tool and reduced to 512px maximum dimensions for the game. The prompts are preserved in `prompts.json`; the game-ready JPEG files are in `public/images/interiors/`. No Replicate calls or voice generation were made for this update.

Saved assets: [market painting](../../public/images/interiors/nigeria-market.jpg), [pottery painting](../../public/images/interiors/nigeria-pottery.jpg), [floral relief](../../public/images/interiors/egypt-floral.jpg), [black/gold marble](../../public/images/interiors/egypt-marble.jpg). [Final generation prompts](prompts.json).

The Nigerian paintings depict everyday market and pottery scenes in southwest Nigeria. The indigo textile accents and shallow woven domestic trays are decorative interpretations, not reproductions of sacred or ceremonial objects. They represent a specific Nigerian visual tradition rather than claiming to represent every culture in Nigeria.

Research references:

- [Smithsonian Marilyn Houlberg Nigeria collection](https://www.si.edu/object/archives/sova-eepa-2005-002): documentation of daily life, markets, textile/clothing, architecture and woodwork in southwest Nigeria.
- [Smithsonian archival catalogue](https://sirismm.si.edu/EADpdfs/ACMA.06-017.pdf): entries for Yoruba women at market and women carrying vegetables in baskets.
- [Princeton University Art Museum: Àdìrẹ ẹlé̩kọ wrapper](https://artmuseum.princeton.edu/art/collections/objects/135166): indigo resist-dyed Yoruba women's wrappers.
- [Denver Art Museum: Yoruba Door Panels](https://www.denverartmuseum.org/en/edu/object/yoruba-door-panels): figurative wood carving and women potters.

The Egyptian ivory floral wall finish follows the user's cream-and-gold relief references. Its original flowers are used as shallow visual wall panels. The original black/gold marble texture uses flowing mineral ribbons, with the existing four tile divisions added at runtime. Neither finish changes wall or floor geometry.
