# Nigerian hallway collection

The ten new original paintings were generated with the **built-in image generation tool**, then reduced to 512×384 JPEGs for the game. The two existing paintings are retained. No Replicate calls were made. The [final prompts](prompts.json) preserve the complete ten-image generation set; the [earlier notes and prompts](../interior-art-327/README.md) cover the original market and pottery paintings and their cultural references.

All game-ready files are saved in `public/images/interiors/`:

Every picture appears once in the hospital. Domestic woven trays, textile panels and serving spoons alternate between the paintings along the two outermost side corridors. The paintings depict ordinary life in southwest Nigeria; the collection is an artistic interpretation of specific local themes rather than a claim to represent every Nigerian culture. Sacred and ceremonial objects are excluded.
