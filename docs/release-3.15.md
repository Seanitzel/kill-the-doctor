# KTD 3.15 — China interactions, fitted uniforms and outdoor sound

## Fixes and changes

- The China uniform cupboard can be opened and closed, including after changing. Its body, moving hinged leaves and extended cupboard drawers have solid collision bounds. Drawer search positions remain outside the furniture. The office doors turn around fixed hinges at a readable, bounded angular speed, with their existing sweep collision checks retained.
- The basement starts with “Finally, you’re here.” This exact new line uses locally generated clips from the existing fictional American voice profiles; other recordings are retained. The first instrument request is separate and delayed eight seconds. A–D can be selected while waiting, with a visible selected state, or handed over when requested. Both clicks and keyboard letters work. The equipment panel is no longer clipped inside the small mission-status stack; this was the main cause of clicks falling through to the canvas. Existing wrong-selection, missed-request, recording and two-mistake rules remain.
- Surgical and guard caps use measured head/eye bounds on each rig. The surgical cap clears the eyes, and the mask is a thin curved surface. The white rectangular artefact was an unbound doctor ID badge positioned on the avatar root. It is now fitted to the shirt and attached to the chest bone. The stolen badge uses the doctor's identity and current fictional hospital.
- Patients wear casual collarless shirts with the existing varied clothing colours, crew/V necklines and sleeve lengths. The disguise uses short-sleeved medical clothing. Complete exposed arms replace the hidden partial arm strips under the original long sleeves.
- Eating uses a smooth lower/raise/hold/lower cycle, a curled utensil grip, coordinated arm/wrist orientation and a spoon following the actual fingers. Fitting now refreshes the complete avatar transforms before measuring: stale transforms on moved/turned avatars could otherwise put the mouth target in the wrong place. Both character rigs share the correction.
- Spit irritation uses softly feathered, translucent patches on the actual skinned neck triangles. It fades in and out, and the scratch pose blends from idle. The approximately 30-second movement restriction is unchanged. Replacing the player's model releases/rebinds the irritation resources.
- The ambulance front has a sloped windscreen, pillars, wipers, mirrors, hood, aligned headlights, grille, bumper and identification panel. Its solid footprint includes the new hood, with separate front/rear extents so it does not obstruct the stretcher unloading route. Ambulance-to-emergency routing was rechecked for all 20 countries.
- Passing cars use new recorded engine and tyre/road loops. The ambulance uses a new recorded real siren cycle. All three source recordings are CC0; provenance and processing notes are in `public/audio/field-recordings-v315.txt`. The three vehicle MP3s total 451,942 bytes. Actual vehicle position/velocity drives spatial panning, distance attenuation, a distance/indoor low-pass filter and restrained Doppler pitch. Car route edges fade before wrapping; separate loop offsets avoid phase-locked cars. Vehicle filters are released with their sample channels.
- Smoke-break aurora curtains are more visible, with slowly changing folds and blended green/blue/purple plus a little pink. Shooting stars cross in front of the player's current view at varying intervals, with a bright head and short trail. Ending the break hides both effects and restores the ordinary night sky. Geometry and particle counts remain bounded.
