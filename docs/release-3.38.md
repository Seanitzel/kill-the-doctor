# KTD 3.38 — equipment, fixed Story missions and encounter fixes

This release applies the latest equipment and gameplay feedback to 3.37. The existing regular outfits, gender-specific premium collections, separate shoe colour, recorded coffee mixing, country names and head-aiming limits are retained.

- Equipment cards show twelve colour raster product renders. Purchases use each item's actual owned level, normalize legacy mask ownership, reject duplicate purchases and charge one level at a time. Lightweight footwear adds visible soles and changes speed/footstep output; the other upgrade effects remain tied to their descriptions.
- Mission failures play one failure cue, including failures that pause the scene. Injection contact animates a physical collapse before showing the result.
- The N95 is a fitted, folded white cup with valve and elastic straps. The cup follows the actual face profile on both rigs. Tattoos share the body's morph animation; premium turtleneck hair is tucked. A physical flashlight follows the left hand with an arm/grip pose.
- The Chinese coffee station is copied to the other Story hospitals and default Freeplay. China's existing station and its arrangement are retained; there is one station per hospital.
- Rescue theatre keys are carried by a fast-moving maintenance clinician on file, meal, bathroom and utility errands. The player must steal the key without the carrier or other NPCs witnessing it; there is no Rescue cupboard-key alternative.
- Rescue basements contain linked rooms, corridors, cover and alternate routes. The 2/4/5 Story doctors in India/Venezuela/Uganda hide, reposition, duck, attack and attempt to escape. A resisting doctor reaching the elevator immediately fails the mission. Freeplay resistance and difficulty configurations remain separate.
- Hidden accomplices are mandatory only in the America (3) and England (5) Story Target chapters. They move between hiding places and use guns, visible syringes or gas. Some Freeplay Target configurations include optional accomplices. Gas impairs movement for 60 seconds without automatic failure; firearm injuries share body-region damage rules.
- Freeplay starts in the original Nhalewick hospital. Completing a Story country unlocks its interior as an optional Freeplay selection; the default remains available. Story exploration access is not treated as completion.
- Target office absence notices are red and follow physical occupancy. Drawing publicly without aiming at an authorized target fails; deliberate target aiming does not fail merely because it is witnessed. The target flees immediately on seeing the gun, and witnesses can warn them. Crowd flight alone is not a failure condition.
- The rota says “Room Clear”, shows visitor caution separately and stops its updates after collection. Discovered Target departure/return information remains available independently and is reset consistently on retry.
- Ordinary lights remain steady in France, China, Russia, America, Romania and England. Nigeria, India, Venezuela, Uganda and the default hospital retain their existing pooled/motion lighting. Power-outage mechanics remain unchanged. The steady implementation evaluates fixed fixtures at each surface with the existing bounded light slots, colours, falloffs, ambient settings and exposure. Its distributed contributions are normalized against the 3.37 rendered view to avoid the brightness changes caused by a sparse fixed-light layout.
- Wall-corner cover stays standing, turns the head toward the corner and latches peeking with a short press. A second press returns to cover. Feet remain planted while aiming and shooting; the cover camera selects the clear shoulder side. Ordinary movement clearance and the ordinary shoulder camera remain unchanged. Cupboard and door cover remain available.
- Office warnings are five seconds apart and the clinician yields the exit. Hallway arrows reflect actual navigable branches. Patients do not receive specialist titles. The detached red pursuit beacon is suppressed; legitimate object and character indicators remain.
- Story seeds, room assignments, target names/appearance and authored encounter configurations are fixed across retries. Freeplay remains randomized. The distressed visitor arrives at 30 seconds in Story chapters 2, 4, 7 and 10, following the latest acceptance checklist.

Validation: 618 unit/regression tests passed; 20 desktop browser checks passed, followed by seven final country-lighting/outage and privacy checks. The iPhone-layout WebKit run passed 17 checks, with the two desktop-only collection/country sweeps intentionally skipped. All eight regular outfit snapshots still match 3.35. The six developed-country comparison views differed by about 1–5% in average brightness; the phone reference differed by about 2%. Outage rendering matched the previous lighting path exactly in these checks. Android and iOS Simulator builds passed, with 1,791 bundled asset hashes verified.
