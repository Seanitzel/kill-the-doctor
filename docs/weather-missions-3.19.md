# Mission weather references

These are authored evening scenes at the named city and a plausible season, not live weather forecasts or statements about an entire country. Rain and thunderstorms are individual weather events, not guaranteed daily conditions. No national climate is represented by a single universal effect.

| Destination | Scene | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| France, Paris | Autumn evening, rain | Météo-France Paris-Montsouris climate normals: https://object.files.data.gouv.fr/meteofrance/data/synchro_ftp/REF_STATION/FICHECLIM_75114001.pdf |
| Nigeria, Lagos | Wet-season evening, thunderstorm | NOAA describes the long rainy season of southern Nigeria and Lagos rainfall: https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/tropical/convergence-zone |
| China, Shanghai | Early-summer evening, rain | Shanghai government describes its monsoon climate and June–July plum rains: https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Overview/20241225/ce046a2a1e754fde8c02804d6d66db18.html |
| India, Mumbai | Monsoon-season golden hour after a shower; light rain persists | Met Office rainy-season overview describes India's summer monsoon: https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/types-of-weather/rain/rainy-seasons |
| Russia, Moscow | Winter evening, falling and settled snow | JMA ClimatView Moscow normals show subzero winter means and winter precipitation: https://www.data.jma.go.jp/cpd/monitor/climatview/graph_mkhtml_nrm.php?m=1&n=27612 . Snow is an authored winter-event inference from these conditions. |
| Venezuela, Caracas | Wet-season golden hour with an approaching thunderstorm | UN OCHA seasonal analysis identifies May–November rains: https://ocha-dap.github.io/ds-teleconnections/enso/ven/ . The particular storm in Caracas is an authored wet-season event. |
| America, New York | Autumn evening, rain | NWS New York monthly temperature/precipitation normals: https://www.weather.gov/okx/LocalClimatologicalData . Snow is possible in winter but this mission is set in autumn. |
| Egypt, Cairo | Dry golden hour through orange sunset to night | EMA/WMO Cairo climatology documents minimal summer rainfall: https://worldweather.wmo.int/en/city.html?cityId=248 |
| Somalia, Mogadishu | Dry-season evening, no snow or rain | FAO SWALIM identifies distinct wet/dry seasons and the warm sunny dry Jilaal season: https://faoswalim.org/water/climate-somalia |
| England, London | Autumn evening, light rain | Met Office Heathrow long-term rainfall averages: https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/climate/maps-and-data/location-specific-long-term-averages/gcpsveqyg |

The India, Venezuela and Egypt scenes use a continuous 150-game-second golden-hour → sunset → night sequence. Exterior directional light, sky/fog color and star visibility follow that same clock. Weather uses fixed particle buffers; snowfall is restricted to the Moscow winter scene, and lightning/sound is restricted to the authored storm scenes. Interior rooms remain readable. All references checked 8 November 2026.
