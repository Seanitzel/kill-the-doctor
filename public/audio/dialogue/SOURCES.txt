Northline ambient dialogue — expressive neural speech revision 3 (2026-10-16)

These clips are locally generated synthetic speech, not human voice-actor recordings. No real person's custom voice clone was created or used. The two short human pain reactions live outside this folder and have separate CC0 provenance in ../expansion-sources.txt.

The 61 unique fictional dialogue lines include everyday conversations, pharmacy questions, clinical examinations, pain requests, startled interruptions, concern about unexplained noises and the fictional recruitment encounter. Both genders are supplied for all lines: 122 text/gender keys and 244 MP3 files. All 56 original text/gender/key mappings are preserved. Caption text remains the key source; spoken renditions use contractions, brief interjections and hesitations. Acamol is spoken as Ah-kah-mol for pronunciation. The manifest records the exact rendition of each take.

Performances:
Female supplied styles af_heart and af_bella; male supplied styles am_michael and am_fenrir. Voice blending, phrase punctuation, clause/sentence pauses and inference pacing differ for conversation, clinical instructions, patients, pain, suspicion, anxiety and reassurance. Each key has KEY.mp3 and KEY-alt.mp3; alternate takes are independently synthesized. Rates range approximately 0.87–1.10. There is no playback pitch shifting, baked room echo, or artificial breath generator. Kokoro does not provide a dedicated emotion-prompt interface; these are prosody/style adjustments, not a claim of human acting or fully controllable emotional expression.

manifest.json retains text/gender/key, category, spokenText, synthetic:true and engine, and records each variant's file, supplied style/blend, delivery, speed, phrase pauses, spoken rendition, duration, bytes and SHA-256. Files are mono 24 kHz, 80 kbit/s MP3. Processing: 70 Hz high-pass, 10.5 kHz low-pass, restrained -20 LUFS / -3 dBTP targets with 11 LU loudness-range allowance and tiny entry/exit padding.

QA:
All dialogue and pain files are decoded and checked for finite nonempty samples and peak headroom. All expected keys and both takes are tested against file SHA-256. This is technical QA, not a claim of a human listening session or actor-grade delivery. Delivery preferences can still benefit from playback feedback.

Reproduce with scripts/generate-dialogue-audio-v2.py (the existing helper now builds revision 3 from ALL_DIALOGUE_LINES). Local models remain in ignored artifacts/audio-v2/models/; revision 3 backups, staging, logs, decoded metrics and short male/female previews are in ignored artifacts/audio-v3/. Original music notes/timing are unchanged; the game raises their bus gain by 20%.
