Northline bundled speech — Pocket TTS revision 4 (2026-10-17)

These clips are synthetic performances of fictional game text, not authentic recordings of real people or medical events. No custom person's voice was cloned for this project. Short human cough, gasp, pain and panic recordings have separate provenance in ../expansion-sources.txt.

74 unique lines are supplied in both genders: 148 text/gender keys and 296 MP3 clips. Every key present in the previous 61-line release is preserved. Text covers everyday, pharmacy and clinical conversations, pain, suspicion, the fictional contact encounter, six short distress remarks, five static hospital public-address announcements, and separate weapon/camera witness warnings. Caption hashes remain exact; spoken renditions use ordinary contractions. Acamol is rendered Ah-kah-mol for pronunciation. Each variant's exact spokenText is recorded in manifest.json.

Generation:
Each entire phrase is synthesized with its supplied preset at the model's recommended sampling temperature 0.3. Alternate takes use independent seeds and preserve the speaker preset. There is no mixed-voice style embedding, forced speech rate, runtime pitch shift or baked reverberation. The goal is more fluid whole-phrase delivery; perceived naturalness and emotion are subjective and are not guaranteed by these technical changes. Six short distress remarks are separate from the human scream cue, allowing an abrupt calm transition controlled by gameplay.

Processing: mono 24 kHz, 80 kbit MP3; 70 Hz high-pass and 10.5 kHz low-pass; restrained -20 LUFS / -3 dBTP targets, 12 LU loudness range allowance; tiny entry/exit padding. manifest.json records text/gender/key, category, synthetic:true, engine/model and each variant's file, supplied voice, exact rendition, seed, temperature, normal speed, duration, bytes and SHA-256. Existing base filenames KEY.mp3 and alternate KEY-alt.mp3 stay compatible.

Five static PA lines now use these bundled recordings. Announcements containing a runtime doctor name and room still use the device's available speech voice, as does the existing failure fallback when a bundled file cannot load. Captions remain available.

QA:
Expected key coverage, both takes, file hashes and whole-file decoding are checked. Each decoded output is finite/nonempty with peak headroom. This is technical QA, not a claim of a human listening session, studio voice acting or guaranteed emotional realism. Short male/female preview montages and decoded metrics are retained in ignored artifacts/audio-v4/ for review.

Reproduce: scripts/generate-dialogue-audio-v4.py with ignored artifacts/audio-v4/venv/. Build-time setup/model downloads are isolated from application dependency manifests and shipped assets. Original music, note timing, machine cues, weapon samples and footstep samples are unchanged; this revision only reduces footstep playback gain.
