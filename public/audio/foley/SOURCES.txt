Hospital foley and weapon audio — source and editing record
Retrieved and prepared 2026-10-16.

No attribution is required. Optional credit: Foley by OwlStorm (Ashe Kirk / Owlish Media), michorvath and moosegravy via Freesound.

Distribution format: mono 32 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV, suitable for browser Web Audio.
Source: each creator's public high-quality MP3 preview linked from the source page; original WAV downloads require a Freesound account. The public previews were converted to WAV and edited; these are not claimed to be original lossless masters.

Footsteps are four distinct isolated hard-shoe recordings. They retain heel/toe contact and a little natural tail; they are not sneaker recordings and do not contain synthesized footfalls. Use subtle runtime room reverb/early reflections for the hospital rather than forcing a long fixed echo into every asset.
Gunshots are field-recorded / creator-described real firearm sounds. They were shortened to the single discharge and its tail, excluding later handling. The rifle retains an early reflected/mechanical transient. No added voices or music. These are game effects, not loudness-calibrated recordings.

Processing: remove leading/trailing dead space; mono downmix; high-pass 70 Hz for steps / 38 Hz for shots; low-pass 13.5 kHz; 2 ms entry and 25 ms exit fades. Footstep peaks -10 dBFS; weapon peaks -4 dBFS. All final WAVs have zero clipped samples.
The pistol HQ MP3 has decoded intersample over-unity peaks; processing used floating-point samples and preserved headroom before the final PCM conversion.

QA: inspected decoded waveforms and spectrograms. Steps have discrete impact/scrape transients and decay; shots have broadband attacks and decaying tails. No sustained speech-like harmonic passages occur in the selected ranges. Spectrograms, source-page snapshots, source previews and exact metrics are in ignored artifacts/audio-qa/ for audit. This was waveform/spectrum inspection, not a claim of a human listening session.
