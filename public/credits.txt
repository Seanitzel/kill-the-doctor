KILL THE DOCTOR — NORTHLINE
Playable private browser prototype, October 2026.

Original hospital geometry, floor layouts, medical furniture, signs, textures,
mission systems, interface, audio synthesis, and character bind-space retargeting
were created for this project.

CHARACTERS
Ready Player Me / Wolf3D avatar sample assets from the three.ws collection:
https://github.com/nirholas/three.ws/blob/main/public/avatars/realistic-male.glb
https://github.com/nirholas/three.ws/blob/main/public/avatars/realistic-female.glb
Local filenames: models/adrian.glb and models/alex.glb.
Runtime modifications: clothing recoloring, skin/hair tinting, role badges,
scaling, and animation retargeting. Avatar rights remain with their creators.
These sample avatars are included for this private, non-commercial prototype.
Ready Player Me's avatar terms apply independently of the repository's software
license. Review the applicable avatar terms before commercial distribution:
https://readyplayer.me/terms
https://studio.readyplayer.me/terms
