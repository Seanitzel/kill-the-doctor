KTD — KILL THE DOCTOR
Playable private browser prototype, October 2026.

Original hospital geometry, floor layouts, medical furniture, signs, textures,
mission systems, interface, audio synthesis, and character bind-space retargeting
were created for this project.

KTD 2.3 voice update: NPC dialogue and fixed PA generated with Kyutai Pocket TTS (runtime MIT, model weights CC BY 4.0). Supplied Alba preset performance by Alba MacKenna, CC BY 4.0; supplied Marius preset is based on the CC0 Unmute Selfie.wav voice donation. Generated recordings are synthetic. Full attribution, source links and license text: audio/dialogue/SOURCES.txt and audio/dialogue/NOTICE-Pocket-Voices-CC-BY-4.0.txt.

KTD 2.4 voice update: NPC dialogue and hospital announcements use four original synthetic voice identities designed with Qwen3-TTS (Apache 2.0), rendered with MLX Audio (MIT). Standard American and Southern/Texas-inspired descriptions guide delivery; accent labels describe the intended performance and may vary. No real-person recording was used to create or condition these identities. Source descriptions, licenses and all 300 clip records: audio/dialogue/SOURCES.txt and audio/dialogue/manifest.json. Earlier model/preset attributions above document superseded recordings. No generation model or reference-voice files are shipped in the game.
