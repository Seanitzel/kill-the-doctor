KTD — KILL THE DOCTOR
Playable private browser prototype, October 2026.

Original hospital geometry, floor layouts, medical furniture, signs, textures,
mission systems, interface, audio synthesis, and character bind-space retargeting
were created for this project.

CHARACTERS
Ready Player Me / Wolf3D avatar sample assets from the three.ws collection:
https://github.com/nirholas/three.ws/blob/main/public/avatars/realistic-male.glb

Clinical and maternity scenes are fictional, clothed, and non-graphic. NPC dialogue is bundled as generated English speech recordings. Public-address
announcements use the browser's installed English speech voices. Recorded cries and vocals
are bundled locally and positioned within the hospital through Web Audio.

Samantha and Daniel text-to-speech voices, converted to mono 22.05 kHz MP3.
The manifest in audio/dialogue/ lists each authored line and voice variant.
